# QA Checklist

All data in this file is synthetic and created for demonstration purposes.

- Is the automated suggestion visible to the reviewer?
- Is the confidence level visible?
- Is there a clear approve/archive/escalate action?
- Are reviewer notes captured?
- Are overrides tracked?
- Are low-confidence records blocked from automatic downstream action?
- Is there an owner for unresolved exceptions?
- Are generated fields separated from final decision fields?
- Is there a regular QA sample review?
